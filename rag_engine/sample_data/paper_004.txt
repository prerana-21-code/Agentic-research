Title: Machine Learning Methods for Disease Prediction

Abstract:
Machine learning techniques are widely applied to predict diseases using patient information and clinical datasets.

Algorithms such as Decision Trees, Random Forest, Support Vector Machines, and Logistic Regression can identify patterns in medical records and estimate disease risk.

Common input features include patient age, blood pressure, glucose levels, cholesterol, and medical history.

Feature selection and data preprocessing are important for improving prediction performance. Class imbalance and missing clinical values remain major challenges in developing reliable disease prediction systems.

Unlike medical image classification, traditional machine learning approaches often work with structured clinical data rather than X-ray or MRI images.
