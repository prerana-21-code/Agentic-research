Title: Natural Language Processing for Biomedical Research

Abstract:
Natural Language Processing (NLP) helps researchers extract useful information from large collections of biomedical documents.

Techniques such as Named Entity Recognition, text classification, keyword extraction, and relation extraction can identify diseases, medications, genes, and medical procedures from scientific literature.

Transformer-based language models can analyse biomedical text and support automated literature reviews.

NLP systems can also identify research trends, summarize scientific findings, and extract relationships between medical concepts.

Challenges include complex medical terminology, limited annotated datasets, and the need to verify automatically extracted information against original research sources.
