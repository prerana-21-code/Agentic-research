Title: Transformer-Based Models for Medical Image Analysis

Abstract:
Transformer architectures have gained attention in medical image analysis because of their ability to capture long-range relationships in visual data.

Vision Transformers divide medical images into smaller patches and process them using self-attention mechanisms. This approach can identify important relationships between different regions of an image.

Transformer-based models have been explored for tumour segmentation, organ detection, and disease classification using MRI and CT scans.

However, these models often require large training datasets and significant computational resources. Combining CNNs with transformers may improve feature extraction and classification performance.
