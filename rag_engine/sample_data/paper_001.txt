Title: Deep Learning Techniques for Medical Image Classification

Abstract:
Medical image classification is an important application of artificial intelligence in healthcare. Deep learning techniques can automatically identify patterns in medical images and support disease diagnosis.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are widely used for analysing X-ray images, MRI scans, and CT scans. CNN models learn visual features from medical images and classify them into different disease categories.

Machine learning techniques can improve classification performance by identifying patterns in medical imaging datasets. Data preprocessing and image augmentation can also help improve model generalisation.

The study highlights the importance of deep learning in medical image analysis and discusses challenges such as limited labelled datasets and model interpretability.
