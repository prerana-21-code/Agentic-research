Title: Deep Learning Approaches for Brain Tumour Detection

Abstract:
Brain tumour detection using magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is an important application of artificial intelligence in medical diagnosis.

Convolutional Neural Networks can automatically extract features from MRI images and classify brain scans into categories such as glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumour, and no tumour.

Image preprocessing techniques such as resizing, normalization, and augmentation help improve the quality of training data.

Deep learning models can support radiologists by identifying suspicious regions in medical images. However, model performance depends on dataset quality, image resolution, and the availability of labelled medical scans.
